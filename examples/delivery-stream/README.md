# Delivery stream instance

One product team's value stream for a fictional note-taking app, from a customer's idea to a change running in production. It shows the engine on a design that has nothing to do with security. Packs: generic, plan, flow, with `flow.max_handoffs: 6`.

- **Model.** 7 teams, each naming its lead by role title (the product lead, the platform engineering lead, the release manager and so on), and 22 elements. Person-steps carry `attrs.wait_days` and queues carry `attrs.wip_limit`. There are 31 relationships: moves between teams are `handoff`, moves inside a team are `work`, and the loops back are `feedback`. 17 contracts, including a change request, a build artifact and a release note. Three views: the stream end to end, the feedback loops, and merge to release.
- **Plan.** Three phases (see the stream, shorten it, keep it short) with 21 units joined by capabilities. Four units are done and one is in progress. The delivery lead is accountable for each, and each names its lead, who owns delivering it: the delivery lead, the platform engineering lead and the product lead. Three units name a lead of their own over their owner team's: “Walk one change from idea to production” (the delivery lead), “Automate the release checklist” (the test automation engineer) and “Draft release notes from merged changes” (the technical writer). every other unit inherits its owner team's. The delivery lead's green on “Shorten it” is an override with a review date, flagged because what it waits on is red.
- **Findings on purpose.** The roadmap backlog has no WIP limit. The path from customers to production crosses 7 team handoffs against a limit of 6.
- **Patch P-001 (proposed).** Folds the weekly release approval into the automated deploy gate. The step, its contract and two handoffs go, and a passing test report goes straight to the deploy service. The impact report shows the handoff finding resolved and no check broken. Its verdict is caution, review before accepting: its why counts on automating the release checklist (U-08) and gating deploys on the suite (U-09), and both are still blocked. An agent wrote it at the delivery lead's request.
- **Patch P-002 (proposed).** Written by hand by a person (`provenance.author: person`, `requested_by: the product lead`): caps the roadmap backlog at fifteen ideas, so triage accepts a new idea only when design pulls one out. It passes every check and fixes two findings (the backlog's missing WIP limit, and “Set a WIP limit on every queue” being done while the map said otherwise). Being proposed, it changes no milestone's colour and leaves P-001's verdict as it was.
- **Glossary.** `engine.yaml` `glossary:` says what a zone means here (where a change stands: before commitment, committed or live) and adds "value stream". every other term on the page comes from the engine.
- **Tour.** 18 steps. The Teams step says each team names its lead and a unit inherits it. The step before the committed zone highlights that band in the stream drawing, and the zone step opens it.

Engine 0.4.1 retains this lone instance's schema 0.3 behavior. It has no namespace, root or pins. Its bundle golden changes only engine.version and engine.built.
